How to use for Short Straddles

  • Positive gamma regime (spot above flip) favors mean-reversion / pinning — better environment for short straddles.
  • Spot close to Max Pain increases pin probability into expiry.
  • Expected Move (1σ) shows the range the market prices. Short ATM straddle profits if price stays inside roughly 0.8× this move (breakevens ≈ ATM ± straddle premium).
  • High ATM IV relative to recent realized (not shown here) makes premium richer for sellers. Watch the 25Δ RR for directional skew.
  • Call/Put walls act as magnets or acceleration points. Prefer selling when walls are not too far and OI is concentrated near ATM.
